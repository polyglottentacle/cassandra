Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

In short: in normal use your chat never leaves your device. Cassandra reads the file in your browser and does the counting there. No account, no ads, no tracking cookies, and we never sell anything about you.

What happens to the file you load

  • Your browser reads the chat file on your phone or computer, and all the counting happens there.
  • In normal use no message, name or photo is sent to us or anyone else.
  • The messages and photos you see stay in memory only while the page is open. Close it and they're gone.

What stays saved on your device

  • Only the numbers of a reading (for example how many messages each person sent) and the names that appear in the chat, so you can find your readings again.
  • Never the text of the messages, never the most-used words, never the photos.
  • You can delete them anytime with "Forget this reading" or by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Invitation program (invite only)

Some people use Cassandra through a personal invitation link. Only if you open such a link and tap "I'm in", and until you tap "Leave", the following is stored in a database hosted in the European Union to improve the app and to answer your questions: the chat you load, the results, the questions you ask, errors, your device type and when you use the app. We keep it only while the invitation program needs it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. Without an invitation, nothing is sent.

The "Ask" feature

The angel and the devil answer with rules that run on your device. If an optional AI helper is turned on for invited users, it receives only your question and the numbers of the chat — never the messages and never the names.

Hosting

The app files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, it may log technical data about the visit (such as your IP address) under its own policy. Cassandra does not receive or use this data.

Other people in the chat

A chat also contains other people's words. Use Cassandra only for chats you took part in, and don't publish other people's messages or photos without their permission.

Children

Cassandra is meant for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child sent us data through an invitation, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights (including California and the EU)

We do not sell or share your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising. If you joined through an invitation, you can ask to see the data stored about you or to have it deleted. California residents have these rights under the CCPA, and people in the EU under the GDPR; we honor them for everyone. Use the contact below.

What Cassandra is not

The verdict is a game with fixed, published rules. It is not psychological or legal advice and proves nothing about a relationship. See the Terms of Use.

Contact

For questions or to exercise your rights, open a request on the project page.

  • Mai il testo dei messaggi, mai le parole più usate, mai le foto.
  • Li cancelli quando vuoi con «Dimentica questo responso» oppure cancellando i dati del sito dal browser.

Solo su invito

Solo in quel caso, e finché non premi «Esci», la chat che carichi, i risultati e le domande che fai vengono conservati (in un database in Europa) per far crescere l'app e risponderti; puoi chiederne la cancellazione. Chi usa Cassandra senza invito non manda niente a nessuno.

Le altre persone nella chat

Una chat contiene anche le parole di altre persone. Usa Cassandra solo per chat tue e non pubblicare frasi o foto altrui senza il loro permesso.
