Terms of Use

Last updated: November 27, 2026

In short: Cassandra is a game. It counts the numbers of a chat and turns them into a playful verdict. It is not advice, and it cannot tell you the truth about a person or a relationship.

1. What Cassandra is

Cassandra is a free entertainment app. You load a chat you exported from WhatsApp; Cassandra counts messages, reply times and similar numbers, and an "angel lawyer" and a "devil detective" comment on them with fixed, published rules. By using Cassandra you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use it.

2. What Cassandra is not

  • It is not psychological, medical, legal, financial or relationship advice.
  • A verdict, a score or a "red flag" proves nothing about a person. Numbers don't know your life.
  • Don't make important decisions — about a relationship, your safety or your health — based only on Cassandra. If you feel unsafe, talk to someone you trust or contact local emergency services.

3. Who can use it

You must be at least 18 years old. Cassandra is not meant for children and is not directed to anyone under 13.

4. Your chats and other people

  • Only load chats you took part in.
  • A chat also contains other people's words. Respect their privacy: don't publish their messages, names or photos without permission.
  • Don't use Cassandra to monitor, track, pressure, harass or shame anyone.

5. Invitations

Some people use Cassandra through a personal invitation. In that case some data is stored to improve the app, as explained in the Privacy Policy. Without an invitation, nothing you load leaves your device.

6. Paid features

Cassandra is free. If paid features are ever offered, the price and what you get will be shown clearly before you pay.

7. No warranty

Cassandra is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind. It may contain mistakes, miscount a chat or stop working. To the fullest extent permitted by law, we are not liable for any loss or damage arising from your use of Cassandra.

8. Changes

We may update these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version. If you keep using Cassandra after a change, you accept the new terms.
